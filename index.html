Creator workflow & channel operations

Plan, organize, review and manage your content in one workspace.

ZOZ Pro Content Center is a workflow service for independent creators and authorized content teams. It brings content planning, media handoff, connected-channel administration and publishing review into one organized workspace.

Open the ZOZ Pro workspace Explore the workflow

What the service provides

The workspace is designed to keep content preparation and account-owner decisions together, while using each platform's own authorization and publishing rules.

Content planning

Prepare topics, scripts, descriptions and publishing notes before a post is sent to a connected platform.

Media organization

Collect video assets and production handoffs in a project-based workspace so content can be reviewed before use.

Connected channels

Connect eligible social accounts through the platform's authorization flow and manage only accounts you own or are authorized to administer.

Publishing review

Review the target account, content and publishing details before taking an external publishing action. Account connection alone does not publish content.

How it works

1. Sign in

Access the workspace using the authorized workspace owner account.

2. Connect an account

Choose a platform and approve the permissions shown by that platform. You can revoke access through the platform where supported.

3. Prepare and review

Organize the media and review captions, destination account and publishing settings.

4. Complete the platform action

Use only the upload or publishing capabilities enabled for the app and the connected account. Some TikTok uploads are delivered to the creator's inbox for final review and posting in TikTok.

Platform permissions and user control

ZOZ Pro requests platform permissions only for enabled features. TikTok access is subject to TikTok app review, approved scopes and the creator's authorization. The current TikTok workflow is intended for upload-to-inbox where available; it does not claim automatic public posting. The account owner remains responsible for final review and any action that publishes content publicly.
