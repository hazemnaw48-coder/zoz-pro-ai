## Research safeguards

- Start with one tailored message to a relevant public business/community contact.
- Ask the moderator/platform to share an opt-in invitation rather than request or scrape member contact details.
- Do not send repeated follow-ups after refusal or silence.
- Do not ask interviewees to upload contracts, client identities, private DMs, payment details, or credentials.
- Use an anonymized interview ID and record only the actual workflow, current workaround, consequence, tools already used, and permission to follow up.
- No paid outreach, ads, list purchase, or payment integration.
- Report outcomes as: sent / delivered / replied / permission to share / interviews completed. Do not mark any as complete without evidence.

## Validation thresholds

- 15 qualified interviews (UGC creators with at least two paid collaborations and rights-based usage).
- At least 8 describe a recent rights-scope, licence-window or invoice follow-up problem.
- At least 5 show a workaround or concrete consequence.
- At least 3 volunteer to test the free prototype.
- Stop or revise if existing tools already solve the problem or if no consistent unmet need appears.
