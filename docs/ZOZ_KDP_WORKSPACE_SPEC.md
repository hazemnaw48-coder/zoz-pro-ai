# ZOZ KDP Workspace — book preparation and submission support

## Scope
Automate book production preparation and status tracking while preserving owner control over Amazon account actions and publishing declarations.

## Automated preparation
- Collect manuscript chapters and normalize styles.
- Run spelling, consistency, formatting, and factual-source checks.
- Create front matter, back matter, table of contents, and metadata drafts.
- Prepare cover brief and source assets.
- Validate trim size, bleed, page count, embedded fonts, image resolution, and export package.
- Produce Kindle and paperback preview checklists.
- Track ISBN/ASIN, price draft, marketplaces, review status, and availability from owner-provided reports.

## External account boundary
- Current production registry indicates no direct KDP connector.
- Do not claim direct KDP reads or submissions.
- Do not store Amazon password, cookies, payment details, tax data, or identity documents in ZOZ.
- Use official account pages in the owner's local browser session only.
- Browser assistance may prepare a draft but must not bypass account checks or review.

## Required approvals
Owner approval required before:
- Uploading manuscript or cover to KDP.
- Submitting a book for review or publication.
- Changing price, territories, rights, or KDP Select status.
- Making tax, payment, identity, or legal declarations.
- Purchasing ISBNs or paid services.

## Acceptance tests
- Manuscript package passes structural validation.
- Cover and interior are independently previewed.
- Metadata and rights declarations are presented in a review screen.
- Submission checklist is saved with timestamp and exact artifact versions.
- No external submission occurs without a one-action approval record.
