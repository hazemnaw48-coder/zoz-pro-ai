Nexa Content Studio is a content workflow tool for creators and channel owners. It helps organize video projects, connect supported social accounts with authorization, and prepare content for review.

How it works

  1. The account owner signs in to the workspace.
  2. The owner chooses to connect a supported social account and authorizes access on that platform.
  3. Video content and publishing details are prepared for the selected channel.
  4. The owner reviews the content and explicitly chooses whether to proceed. Connecting an account does not itself publish content.

TikTok integration is subject to TikTok's product availability, approved scopes, and app review. Features that have not been approved or configured are not represented as available.

Account and data controls

Access tokens and application data are handled by the service backend. Users should only connect accounts they own or are authorized to manage. Users can request help with access or deletion using the contact route shown inside the service account where available.
