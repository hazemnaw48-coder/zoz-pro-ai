Client-first principles

Control stays with the client

Advertising accounts and payment methods remain under the client's ownership and control. Campaign launches, budget changes, payments and material permission changes require explicit client authorization. We do not guarantee specific advertising results.

We do not ask clients to send passwords or one-time codes by email. Access should be granted through official platform permission tools using the minimum necessary role.
