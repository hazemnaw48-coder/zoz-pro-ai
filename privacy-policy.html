Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

This policy describes how ZOZ Pro Content Center (“ZOZ Pro”, “we”, “our”) handles information when users access the content workflow service.

Information processed

  • Account information needed to authenticate the workspace user.
  • Content details supplied by the user, such as video files, titles, captions, prompts, and publishing selections.
  • Social-platform account identifiers and authorization data when the user chooses to connect an account.
  • Technical and operational records needed to secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the service.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, connect accounts at the user's request, store and organize content projects, prepare authorized publishing actions, display task results, and protect the service from misuse.

Platform authorization and publishing

Connecting a social account requires the account owner to authorize the requested permissions on that platform. Platform data is used only for the features and permissions available to the application. Content is not intentionally published merely because an account is connected; the service is designed for owner review and approval before a publishing action is initiated. Availability depends on platform approval and configuration.

Storage and service providers

Application data is processed by the service's backend and hosting/storage providers, including Supabase where configured. Information may be transmitted to a social platform when the user requests an authorized action. We do not sell personal information. We do not place social-platform client secrets in public source code or browser pages.

Retention and deletion

Information is retained only as needed to provide the service, maintain security and records, and meet applicable obligations. Users may request deletion of account-related data and stored content through the service's available support/contact route. Disconnecting a platform account or revoking authorization may prevent further use of that integration; it does not necessarily delete data already stored in the workspace or on the platform, so deletion may need to be requested separately.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Users should keep credentials private and connect only accounts they are authorized to manage.

Children

The service is a general content-management tool and is not intended to collect personal information directly from children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the service changes. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact hazemnaw48@gmail.com.

ZOZ Pro Content Center is an independent service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok. Data handled through TikTok integrations is also subject to TikTok's applicable policies and terms.
